Privacy

Tintlife (Leigh H. Parker, sole proprietor, Timmins, Ontario) collects only what's needed to fill your Tintlife TruEdge order.

What we collect

  • Name, shipping address, email and phone, to ship your order and contact you about it.
  • Engraving text, if you order engraving.
  • Payment is handled by our payment processor (Square); we never see or store your full card number.

How long we keep it

  • Engraving text is deleted 6 months after delivery.
  • Order records are kept as long as Canadian tax law requires.

What we don't do

  • No marketing emails and no mailing list. We only email you about your order.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies on this site.
  • We don't sell or rent your information.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information (subject to legal record-keeping) by emailing tintlifetimmins@gmail.com. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
